In my hometown, where the river Ganga brings forth the festival called Maha Kumbh every 12 years which is the largest human gathering in history. Where the tempratures reach 48 degrees in the summer and the people are more advanced than you might think, there was a school called DPS or Delhi Public School. 

This school is a branded franchise of schools all across India, offering students a balanced and fun education in a professional yet loving manner by including an engaging curriculum with tons of extra activities. 

I gained proper consciousness, access to the internet and admission to this school all the age of 6 years old. 

The place where I began to write my story with school days turning into weekly monday tests, turning into different seasons of the school year like the Summer Vacations, the Half-Yearly and Final Exams, Teacher's Day, Children's Day, and of course the DJ Night before Christmas. 

This place did not just teach me English, Hindi, Maths, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, History, Civics, Economics, Art, Music, Cricket, Football and most importantly COMPUTERS... 
It also taught me how to make friends, fall in love, learn from your mentors, respect your seniors and guide your juniors. 

I can never say enough good things about it, 
I miss my school days. But, don't we all ?
