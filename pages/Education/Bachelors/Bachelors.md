Located on the outskirts of Mohali is one of the most popular universities in India. With a large majority of the student population studying Computer Science. There were 8 semesters with each one having 6-10 subjects covering a many different fields like Digital Electronics, Programming Languages, Advanced Mathematics/Physics, Data Structures/DBMS, Optimization Techniques, Networking, Microprocessors, Graphics, Artificial Intelligence/ML, Operating Systems, Mobile App Development, Software Project Management, Modelling and Simulations, Computer Vision, Real Time Systems, Blockchain and many more.

This is the place where a boy became a man and learned how hard you have to work to give some sort of meaning to your existence. 

It was genuinely a mindblowing experience which sadly only lasted for about 9 month before the world was hit the global pandemic COVID-19. Forcing us to continue our college years from home. 

While this was a major setback to all of us, I learned how to use this pain to motivate myself to never give up and work hard in even the most difficult circumstances. Even through the darkest days, this fire burns always. 

My final year project for Chandigarh University was called "Evaluation of Subjective Answers using Natural Language Processing". An updated version 2.0 of this project is listed on this website where I take it even further by building a whole webapp around it.
