On the South-West side of the greatest city in the world, along the Thames River, is a highly respected institution where I am completing my formal education. 

I study at the Penryhn Road Campus near the Kingston Town Center. Equipped with world class resources like highly advanced computer labs and a multi-million pound Library building. 
My course offers the following subjects : 
- Agile Project Development
- Software Architecture and Programming 
- Software Quality Engineering 
- Business in Practice

My MSc dissertation tackles a problem every engineering team feels but few can measure: where, exactly, software gets stuck on its way to release. I'm building the **Lean Pipeline Bottleneck Quantification (LPBQ) framework**, a data-driven way to find, measure, and rank bottlenecks in Agile delivery pipelines and translate them into concrete fixes. It pulls real workflow history from tools like Jira, GitHub, and Azure DevOps, then applies process mining (PM4Py), statistical process control, and Lean flow metrics (cycle time, lead time, throughput, flow efficiency) to pinpoint the stages that quietly drain a team's speed. What I like most about it is that it sits right on the seam between engineering and management: the same analysis that tells a developer which stage to automate also gives a manager a Power BI dashboard to decide where to invest. The end goal is simple: turn gut-feel guesses about "what's slowing us down" into evidence, and use that evidence to cut delivery time.
